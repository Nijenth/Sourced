Website widget

A chat bubble for your own site. It answers only from the documents you pick below.

1. Add it to your site

Paste this just before </body> on every page that should show the chat.

<script src="https://cdn.sourced.example/widget.js"
        data-site-key="pk_live_brightleaf_7Q2x" async></script>

The site key is public. It only works on the websites listed below, and it can't upload or delete anything.
